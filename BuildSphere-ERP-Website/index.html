GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

BuildSphere is being shaped as a configurable construction operations platform. Here are a few useful things to know.

Is BuildSphere ERP available now?＋

This website is the product's static concept and introduction. Functional ERP modules will be developed in phases.

Can it support multiple projects and sites?＋

Yes. Multi-project and multi-site management are core requirements for the planned platform.

Will teams be able to use it on mobile?＋

The website is responsive, and mobile-friendly ERP workflows are part of the product roadmap.

Can the system be customized?＋

Future configuration will be planned around company structure, workflows, approval levels and reporting needs.
